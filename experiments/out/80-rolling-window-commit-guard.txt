=== what this establishes ===
 `git diff` compares TRACKED paths. A window the fold step has just
   created is untracked in every one of its files, so the old guard
   reads it as 'unchanged', the step exits 0 having printed nothing,
   and the folded window is discarded under a green tick.

 It is the MIRROR IMAGE of the git_dirty defect fixed the same day in
   crates/alloc-bench/src/envinfo.rs, where `git status --porcelain`
   counted untracked files it should have ignored. Here the untracked
   files are the entire change. Both directions of one confusion.
