=== what this probe cannot tell you ===
- WHICH property of the machine anything depends on. It measures how
  much of the ordering is decidable at all, which is the question that
  has to be answered before 'which property' is a well-posed one.
- anything about a second host. Every number above is this machine.
- whether a pair this host leaves unsettled is unsettled everywhere. A
  quieter machine may separate it; that needs the machine.
