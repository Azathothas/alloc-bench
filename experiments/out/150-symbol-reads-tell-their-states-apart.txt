=== what this establishes ===
 `cmd 2>/dev/null | grep -q` reads GREP's exit code. A tool that
   failed to run reads exactly like a subject that lacks the thing
   being looked for, and the second is what gets written down.
 finish and finish_so now read the table ONCE, exit 2 when they
   cannot read it at all, and say 'NOTHING AT ALL' in words rather
   than printing a header over an empty list.
 And one of the four checks failed OPEN rather than closed: the
   prefixed-mode assertion asks whether malloc IS defined, so a
   broken nm answered 'no' and let the archive through. That one
   would not have failed a build; it would have made two mechanisms
   the same experiment and published them as two.

- whether an exported malloc actually interposes. Nothing a build step
