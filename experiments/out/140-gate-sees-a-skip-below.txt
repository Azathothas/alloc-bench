=== what this establishes ===
 A gate that counts only the skips IT decided cannot see a skip one
   level down. Two checkers retired a check each and the summary line
   read '0 skipped' -- under --strict, whose entire job is to refuse a
   skip. The check a session ran before pushing was weaker than the
   one CI would run over the same tree.

 Two independent guards now, on purpose. --strict is passed DOWN to
   every checker that has it, so the gate runs CI's own invocation;
   and a passing check's output is scanned for a skip marker, which is
   what covers a checker with no such flag.

=== what this probe cannot tell you ===
- whether every checker the gate runs has been audited for internal
  skips. alloc-runner selftest also skips and exits 0; it has nothing
  to skip today, and the marker scan is what would catch it if it did.
- whether CI is right. CI already passed --strict; this probe is about
  the gate having drifted below it.
