=== what this establishes ===
 preload-check distinguishes a library the loader took from one it
   did not, and its negative control CAN fail -- case 3 makes it fail.
 Without case 3 the pair would be indistinguishable from a check that
   always answers yes to the positive and no to the control, which is
   what the first draft of preload.rs did.

=== what this probe cannot tell you ===
- whether the mapped library actually SERVED the allocations. The
  stand-in here deliberately does not define malloc. finish_so asserts
  the dynamic export of malloc on the real recipes; the two checks
  together are the argument and neither alone is.
- anything about musl. This host is glibc; the alpine cells exercise
  the musl loader and are the real test.
