=== what this example establishes ===
 On glibc, LD_PRELOAD replaces the allocator in an unmodified dynamic
   binary, and residency proves it did.
 The static libc.a surgery that works on musl does NOT transfer to
   glibc, and the reason is visible above: the member defining malloc
   defines a great deal else that the rest of libc needs.
 This is why this project's planner marks glibc static-replacement cells
   unsupported with that reason rather than letting them fail every run.
