=== what this example establishes ===
 The libc.a surgery is NOT a property of musl alone. It needs a libc
   whose allocator members are referenced only by allocation code.
   Chimera's are referenced by pthread_create and __init_tls, so the
   mechanism is unsupported here -- with a named reason, checked by a
   guard that section 7 shows firing.
 What DOES carry across all three musl distributions is the derivation:
   find libc.a by asking the compiler, read the member list out of the
   archive, refuse on evidence. Two of those three answered differently
   here and the script still did the right thing.
 And a result that costs nothing: a Chimera static binary is already a
   mimalloc binary. The verification above is the whole job.
 With one caveat this example refuses to hide: on Chimera no ELF check
   can tell YOUR mimalloc from the DISTRIBUTION'S. They are the same
   implementation, so the identity oracle every other cell here relies on
   has nothing to discriminate with.
