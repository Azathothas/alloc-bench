=== what this example establishes ===
 The libc.a surgery is a MUSL technique and not an Alpine one: the same
   script, unchanged, works on a distribution that shares no packaging,
   no toolchain configuration and no patch set with Alpine.
 It also shows the honest handling of a toolchain difference: the link
   mode is probed and reported rather than assumed, because a non-PIE
   binary published under a PIE label is the exact failure this
   repository's identity oracle exists to prevent.
